## What the Directory is

The **Directory** view (sidebar → Directory) shows everyone who has an account on this server — not your personal contacts. It's the answer to "who else is here, and what's their role?"

It does not pull from your contact list. People appear here because they have an account on this server, whether or not you have them in Contacts.

## What each row shows

- Avatar (initials, with a color derived from the name).
- Name (first + last).
- Email address.
- A colored **role badge**: Owner, Admin, Member, or Guest.

Role colors:

- **Owner** — primary color
- **Admin** — info color (typically blue)
- **Member** — success color (typically green)
- **Guest** — warning color (typically amber)

Roles are set by an owner or admin under **Settings → Members**.

## Searching the Directory

The search box at the top filters by name or email — substring match, no prefix needed. This box only filters the Directory; it isn't the contact search, and the cross-app search palette doesn't include the Directory.

## Adding someone from the Directory to your contacts

The Directory is read-only — it doesn't include a "Save to Contacts" action. To add a person to your personal address book, copy their email or name and use **+ Create contact**.

## Privacy

Everyone with an account on this server can see the Directory, including each person's role. Your personal contacts are never shown here — they stay private to you.
